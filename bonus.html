Superhero Team: Mindful Eagle & Mint

Mindful Eagle is a hyper-focused avian guardian who uses supreme mental focus and acute vision to detect danger from miles away. His sidekick, Mint, uses refreshing botanical energy to soothe allies and confuse enemies with overpowering aroma gusts.

Foes

    • Minor Superpower: Makes butterflies appear out of thin air
  • Shadow Freeze

    • Minor Superpower: Glowing frost fingertips in the dark
