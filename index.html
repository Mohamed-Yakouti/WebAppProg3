How to Make French Press Coffee

  1. Boil water in a kettle.
  2. Prepare the coffee grounds:
    1. Measure 2 tablespoons of medium-ground coffee.
    2. Place the grounds into the French press.
  3. Pour the hot water over the coffee grounds.
  4. Steep for 4 minutes and press down the plunger.
